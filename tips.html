Tips to Remember

Strategy for Exams

1: Optimal sleep time for me is around 8 hours, with absolute minimum of around 4 hrs.

2: Start studying big tests several days in advance, even if briefly each day.

3: Attend tutorials or review sessions — they force your brain to engage.

Tips 1 - 3 are General


Tests-Preparation:

4: The Big Picture & Organisation is the key.

5: Create a study schedule (even imperfect). Listing everything prevents spending too much time on early topics and rushing critical material later.

6: Look at previous tests BEFORE you begin studying. They reveal how the professor thinks. Observe question patterns first — don’t solve immediately.

7: Reading ≠ Replicating. Knowing the formula ≠ Being able to apply it.
“What I cannot create, I do not understand.”

8: Study alone first. Near the end, collaborate — teaching others exposes understanding gaps and reinforces memory.

9: Work with weaker students; explaining concepts to them strengthens your mastery.

10: Meet the professor one-on-one — they often give critical insights not said in class.

11: Study well in advance. The brain needs time to absorb. Allocate ~3 days for midterms & ~6 days for finals.

12: In emergencies, an energy drink can help — it’s chemistry, not magic.

13: For math: EXERCISE > READING. Doing problems reveals exactly what you don’t understand.

14: Study in a place where others are studying if you slip into laziness 😜.
